Historical Analogs and Lessons

The closest analog is late 2018, when a Fed hiking cycle collided with tight financial conditions and tech-led drawdowns; the mechanism is policy-rate pressure on long-duration assets, not an identical macro backdrop. The critical difference is that 2026 shows strong GDP at 3.4% SAAR and calm credit at 3.26% HY OAS, whereas 2018 saw a faster credit deterioration. A 1994-style rate-of-change shock is a secondary analog: the mechanism is duration repricing from a hawkish Fed, not the absolute yield level (1994 10Y was materially higher than 5%). The 2011 analog is misapplied if it claims sentiment below 50; headline Michigan was ~55.8 in August 2011, not below 50. The non-comparable part in all cases is the AI capex cycle and energy-transition dynamics, which have no historical precedent.
