Historical Analogs and Lessons

1994 (rate-of-change/duration shock, not absolute yield): the mechanism - a rapid repricing of the long end driven by supply and inflation surprises - rhymes with 2026, but 1994 10Y was materially higher than today's ~4.9%, so absolute-level comparisons mislead. Lesson: duration, not level, drives equity drawdowns.

2011 (debt-ceiling / fiscal shock): Michigan sentiment fell to ~55.8 in Aug 2011 but did NOT break below 50; the current 48.1 (Sep) is worse on headline but the mechanism differs (inflation, not fiscal brinkmanship). Lesson: sentiment alone is not a trigger; price/flow evidence drives stance.

2018 (credit-led equity): qualitative comparison only - credit leads equity by an unsourced lag. Current credit (HY 326bp) is widening while equities hold, consistent with the 2018 pattern; treat as a watch, not a forecast.
