Historical Analogs and Lessons

1994 is the relevant analog for the rates shock: a rapid rate-of-change and duration shock, not an absolute yield level comparison, since 1994 yields were materially higher than today. The mechanism is a fast repricing of the long end that compresses equity duration before earnings adjust.

2011 is useful for sentiment: Michigan sentiment in August 2011 was about 55.8, not below 50, so do not confuse headline levels with expectations subcomponents. The lesson is that sentiment can stay depressed while equities recover if earnings hold.

2018 offers a qualitative credit-led-equity comparison only, since the lead time is not independently sourced. The lesson is that credit stress can precede equity weakness by months, but the timing is unreliable.
