Historical Analogs and Lessons

The closest analog is 1994: a rate-of-change shock where the level of yields mattered less than the speed of repricing, and duration assets suffered while cyclicals and value held up. The critical difference is that the 10-year yield in 1994 was materially higher than today's 4.93 percent, so the absolute level is not the trigger; the term premium is. A second analog is 2018, where credit led equity only qualitatively; we do not assume a fixed lead time. Michigan sentiment at 48.1 is weak but we treat it as corroborating context, not a primary driver, and note it is September 2026 data.
