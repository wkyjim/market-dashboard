CIO View

Market internals validate a risk-on bias: 76.67% of tracked constituents are above the 50-day average and 76.27% above the 200-day average, while core equity ETFs remain above key moving averages. The 13 August official close broadened the advance: the S&P 500 closed at 7,798.99 (+0.65%), the Nasdaq Composite at 26,803.03 (+0.81%) and the Russell 2000 at 3,052.85 (+0.24%). The 14 August 18:11 HKT live snapshot showed VIX 14.56, 10Y/30Y 4.66%/5.23%, WTI $81.80 and gold $4,413; these are not U.S. cash closes. Elevated long yields and limited copper/oil confirmation retain a discount-rate and growth-scare vulnerability. Hold a measured equity overweight tactically, funded from Utilities and rate-sensitive Real Estate; reduce beta if breadth breaks or inflation re-accelerates.

Short-Term View — 1–3 Months

1–3M Overweight US equities, concentrating active risk in Financials, Health Care, Industrials and selected Technology/Cybersecurity. The broad advance and positive XLF/XLV/XLI flow structures are supportive. Require a close above SPY’s 20-day average (750.17) and breadth above 60%; otherwise move to neutral.

Long-Term View — 6–12 Months

6–12M Neutral US equities. Earnings and productivity can extend the expansion, but elevated long-duration yields increase the hurdle for multiple expansion. Prefer profitable, cash-generative franchises and health-care/industrial earnings breadth over unprofitable growth or rate-sensitive defensives.
