Historical Analogs and Lessons

1994 Analog — Rate-of-Change / Duration Shock: The current environment resembles 1994 in terms of rate-of-change and duration shock — not absolute yield level. 1994 saw the 10Y rise from ~5.6% to ~8.0% in a year. Current 10Y at 5.11% is materially lower in absolute terms. The comparable mechanism is the speed of repricing, not the level. Non-comparable part: absolute yield levels (1994 10Y was ~300bps higher at peak), and the starting level of equity valuations differs significantly.
2011 Analog — Headline Sentiment vs Subcomponents: In August 2011, headline University of Michigan sentiment was ~55.8 — it did not fall below 50. Confusing headline with subcomponents (expectations, current conditions) can lead to erroneous conclusions. The current Michigan reading of 44.8 is from May 2026 and is stale — it should not be used as a current market trigger.
2018 Analog — Credit-Led Equity: The Q4 2018 correction was preceded by credit-market stress (HYG peaked in Nov, equity fell in Dec). We note this as a qualitative comparison only — the specific lead time between credit stress and equity drawdown is not independently sourced and should not be presented as a precise timing tool.
2022 Analog — Duration Repricing: The 2022 rate-hike cycle caused TLT to fall ~30% and SPX ~25%. The current 10Y move is sharp (+15bps/day) but from a lower base. Non-comparable: the Fed was actively hiking in 2022; today the Fed is on hold at 3.63% effective funds rate.
