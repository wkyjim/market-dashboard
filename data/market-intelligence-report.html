Historical Analogs and Lessons

1994-style rate-of-change shock: the mechanism is a rapid repricing of the long end on supply and inflation news, not the absolute 5% yield level (1994 10Y was materially higher). The comparable part is duration compression in growth equities; the non-comparable part is today's higher starting debt load and weaker breadth.

2011-style sentiment washout: headline Michigan sentiment was ~55.8 in August 2011, not below 50; the expectations subcomponent was the weak leg. Today's 48.1 September Michigan reading is weaker, but it corroborates rather than drives the stance, which rests on price, flow and breadth.
