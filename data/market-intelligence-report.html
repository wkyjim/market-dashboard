CIO View

The regime held at Mild Risk-On at 59.11/100 as the S&P 500 and Nasdaq advanced on 18 September and VIX fell to 14.81. The market is rewarding profitable growth and thematic leaders, not broad cyclicality: only 45% of constituents are above their 50-day average, equal weight lost 0.48%, small caps fell 0.50%, and modeled IVV/HYG/LQD flows remain distributive. Keep broad beta neutral, add risk through Cybersecurity, quality growth and selective Health Care, and retain Energy/gold as inflation hedges rather than chase crude-linked momentum. Upgrade only when equal weight, small caps and credit confirm; cut gross if the 10-year holds above 5.1% or breadth reverses below 40%.
