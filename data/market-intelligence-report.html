Historical Analogs and Lessons

The 1994 analog is the relevant comparator for the rates shock: a rate-of-change and duration shock rather than an absolute yield level, since 1994 10-year yields were materially higher than today's 4.91%. The mechanism is a fast repricing of the Fed path compressing equity duration, not the level itself.

The 2011 analog applies to the sentiment channel: Michigan sentiment in August 2011 was around 55.8, not below 50, so the current 48.1 reading (September 2026) is weaker than 2011 and should not be read as the same episode. The current reading is a corroboration of stress, not the driver of the stance.

The 2018 analog is useful only qualitatively for credit-led-equity dynamics; we do not rely on a specific lead time. Credit remains calm at 3.26% HY spread, which argues against an imminent systemic risk-off.
