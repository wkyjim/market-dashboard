CIO View

Maintain Neutral aggregate US-equity beta into the next inflation prints, paired with active overweights in Financials, Health Care and Industrials. The 6–12 month stance remains Overweight: breadth, price trend and contained VIX validate earnings exposure, but returns should be expected to come from earnings delivery and dispersion rather than broad multiple expansion.

Short-Term View — 1–3 Months 1–3M

Trend is constructive: S&P 500 closed 7,757.64 (+0.62%) and Russell 2000 +1.10% on 7 Aug; 73.33% of tracked constituents were above 50DMA. Neutral beta reflects the conflicting signal from gold (+3.76%), 10Y yield at 4.65%, and oil/geopolitical headlines. Increase beta only if CPI/PPI are benign and breadth/credit remain firm.

Long-Term View — 6–12 Months 6–12M

Constructive, conditional on profits absorbing the still-high long-rate discount rate. Favor profitable cyclicals and quality growth with pricing power. A durable 10Y break above 4.9%, renewed HY deterioration, or breadth below 60% would reduce the strategic overweight.

Price Action, Breadth and Momentum

Primary and intermediate trends are positive: the dashboard records equity-trend 93.75/100, 73.33% above 50DMA and 72.88% above 200DMA. Momentum is more measured (52.71/100), and 20-day sector dispersion is 8.36 points; therefore, use technical strength to select exposures rather than as an index-level return forecast. Equal weight outperformed cap weight by 0.28 points over 20 days, while growth lagged value by 3.82 points and small caps lagged SPY by 0.54 points—evidence of broadening, not a confirmed small-cap breakout.
