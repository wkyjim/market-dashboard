Critical difference: 1994 started from a lower inflation base; today core PCE at 3.12% YoY is already above target. Lesson ST: avoid long-duration growth; LT: quality and cash-flow compounders recover once the terminal rate is visible.

2011 analog: headline Michigan sentiment was ~55.8 in August 2011 and did NOT fall below 50; do not confuse with expectations subcomponents. Current Michigan 48.1 (September 2026 observation, stale) is weaker than the 2011 headline but is a single stale measure and corroborates rather than drives the stance. Critical difference: 2011 was a confidence shock without a concurrent inflation re-acceleration; today inflation is the binding constraint. Lesson: sentiment alone does not justify a risk-off pivot; price/flow evidence does.
