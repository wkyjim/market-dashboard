Short Interest
Outstanding short positions reported on the FINRA schedule.
Short Volume
Daily short-sale transaction activity reported to FINRA facilities; it is not a change in outstanding short interest.
SVR
Short Volume divided by Total FINRA Volume.
CASV
Cumulative abnormal SVR relative to the stock's strictly lagged historical baseline.
Funding Short Score
Inferred likelihood that behavior resembles a persistent funding short.
Funding Short Quality
Liquidity, stability, relative weakness and lower squeeze-risk suitability.

Potential reversal based on published SI, relative performance and technical confirmation.
