## Macro View

### Short-Term Macro View

Labor has become the key transmission channel: July payrolls fell 23k and unemployment was 4.1%, while participation eased to 61.4%. This is equity-positive only if disinflation permits policy easing; it is equity-negative if it precedes earnings downgrades. June inflation records are stale but mixed: core PCE +3.29% y/y, core CPI +2.81% y/y and core PPI +4.68% y/y. Lower WTI helps, but the 30-year yield at 5.20% signals persistent fiscal/term-premium pressure. Bull case: softer labor lowers rates without impairing demand. Bear case: weak hiring meets sticky prices. Confirmation: July CPI/PPI, claims, and credit spreads. **Confidence: 6/10.**

### Long-Term Macro View

The base case is slow nominal-growth expansion rather than a recession: June retail sales and housing starts improved, but mortgage rates were 6.49% and consumer sentiment was 44.8. Manufacturing/services, productivity, business capex and fiscal financing determine whether margins can offset the rate burden. China, Europe and Japan dashboard observations are 2025-dated and are not decision-grade. Policy and tariff/geopolitical effects are treated as tail-risk amplifiers, not base-case forecasts. **Confidence: 5/10.**
