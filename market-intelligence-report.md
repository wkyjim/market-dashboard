Europe/China/Japan dashboard structural series are mostly 2025 and are not used as current signals. The bullish interpretation is a nominal-growth/industrial-investment cycle; the bearish interpretation is a fiscal-supply and inflation premium that suppresses multiples. Confidence 5/10.

Lesson: retain hedges. These are contextual analogs, not forecasts (confidence 4/10).
